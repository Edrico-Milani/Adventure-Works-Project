## Description
Adventure Works, a multinational bicycle manufacturer, has multiple sales operations across the globe.
The company’s executive board and sales management team require quarterly sales information to assess their performance for the previous quarter.
This project aims to provide them with a sleek Power BI dashboard that turns $2.9M in global sales into sharp, strategic insights. It highlights product
category dominance (with Bikes driving 83% of sales), US market strength ($2.39M), and seasonal revenue
patterns across months.

From regional performance to sales rep distribution and order quantity by country, this dashboard helps
business leaders make smart decisions fast, on people, products, and markets.

Key Questions This Dashboard Answers:

Sales Director: “Which reps or regions need coaching vs. recognition?”
VP of Product: “Are we overly reliant on bike sales? What’s the untapped potential in other categories?”
Regional Manager: “Is North America truly outperforming, or are there hidden trends in Europe or Pacific
markets?”
Finance Lead: “Where can we optimize costs in low-margin months without hurting performance?”
Marketing Manager: “Which countries buy more units but generate lower revenue—pointing to promo-heavy
behavior?”
